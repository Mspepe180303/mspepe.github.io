Desert Botanical Garden

Desert Botanical Garden began in 1939; it was a grassroots effort to save the natural plant life found in our deserts. Since then, they’ve grown and display and protect over 50,000 different plants. They host educational events to teach about these plants, and funding goes to research to improve our impact on native desert plant life.

Arizona Native Plant Society

Founded in the fall of 1976, now with eleven chapters found across Arizona their goal is to educate the masses on the wonderful plants that make up our desert ecosystem. To achieve this goal, they host their own newspaper, “Plant Press Arizona.” They also host workshops, meetings, and events to help promote and do their part in protecting Arizona’s beautiful plant life.

Central Arizona Conservation Alliance

Created in 2012 as an initiative by the Desert Botanical Garden, the Central Arizona Conservation Alliance approaches plant conservation by protecting Arizona’s wide and natural land. By creating protected and safe land for plants to grow on naturally they can encourage education, exploration, and relaxation.
