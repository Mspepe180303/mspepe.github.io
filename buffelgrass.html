Buffel Grass (Pennisetum Ciliare)

Buffel Grass (Pennisetum ciliare) is a grass native to Africa, Asia, and Middle east of Europe. They were popular as Caddle feed because of their rich and plentiful growth and quick production. However, because of the quick growth, far spreading seeds and drought tolerance. They have taken over much of Arizona’s land. Out competing native plants for resources and starving them. They have been noted as causing a problem in several US states and territories.
