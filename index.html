About Us:

Arizona Botanicals was founded by an ASU student named Matthew Pepe. My goal in this project was to bring more awareness to Arizona's bountiful plant life. by creating educational content for the avergae person I hope to share the love of plants with my neighbors in Arizona. Spreading awareness and support for our plant life can go a long way to keeping Arizona healthy and thriving as it was meant to be.
