Tree of Heaven (Ailanthus altissima)

Tree of Heaven (Ailanthus altissima) originates from China and introduced to the US in the 1700s. Because of their large plumage and crowding it takes up wide amount of space blocking and even killing native plants by out competing them. Because of their fast growth and abundant seeds, they have become a major issue, especially in the western US.
