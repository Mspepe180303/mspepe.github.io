Yellow Star Thistle (Centaurea solstitialis)

The Yellow Starthistle is native to Southern Europe, they thrive in dry and disturbed climates. They tend to bunch and crowd together; this causes them to take up more space from native plants. A big drawback they have is their thorns, because they grow in large groups, they prevent native animals from crossing and creating large barriers.
